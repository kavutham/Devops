What Went Wrong
- The address field was not loading when users navigated back to the previous page.
- The issue was caused by a QAS (Quick Address Search) service failure, which is the backend addresslookup engine.
- A Node.js logic bug prevented the address component from reloading correctly after page navigation.
" What is QAS?
- QAS (Quick Address Search) is the addressvalidation and autocomplete engine used to fetch and validate customer addresses.
- If QAS fails, address fields do not populate or validate correctly.
" How We Identified the Issue
- Detected through Splunk error reports showing repeated QAS lookup failures.
- Analysed customer impact, error patterns, and confirmed only 10"15 customers were affected out of ~1000.
- Also matched with a known error pattern where the system rejects PO Box"only addresses.

What Was the Fix
- Backend remediation team applied a Node.js fix to handle the QAS response correctly and reload the address field.
- Customer contact team performed manual remediation for impacted customers until the fix was deployed.
- Issue was included in the weekly remediation release.
Why It Reached Production (Gap)
- There was a gap in test cases " the scenario of navigating back to the previous page and reloading the address field was not covered in QA.
- QAS failure scenarios (e.g., PO Boxonly addresses) were not fully tested in lower environments.
- QAS integration tests in UAT did not include negative or edgecase address patterns.

RTO " Recovery Time Objective
- How long it takes to restore a service after an outage.

RPO " Recovery Point Objective
- How much data loss is acceptable during an incident.

" Certificate Renewal
- Performed proactive certificate monitoring and renewal to prevent service outages, ensuring all internal and external endpoints remained compliant and secure.
Patching (Minor " Automated, Major " Manual)
- Managed automated minor patch cycles and coordinated major patching activities across infrastructure and applications, ensuring stability, compliance, and minimal downtime.
- Application & Database Restoration
- Executed app and DB restoration procedures during incidents or DR drills, validating backup integrity and ensuring recovery objectives (RTO/RPO) were consistently met.
Disaster Recovery (DR)
- Participated in DR planning and execution, validating failover processes, documenting gaps, and ensuring systems met business continuity requirements.
" Annual Capacity Planning
- Conducted annual capacity assessments by analysing infra response times, app performance, CPU/memory utilisation, and traffic patterns.
- Collaborated with business teams to forecast future load; marked capacity as green/amber based on projections and planned scaling where required.
- Compared current performance with previous year benchmarks to ensure growth readiness and performance consistency.
" Currency Upgrades (Frameworks, NEF, NABServ, etc.)
- Performed currency uplift for platform components, frameworks, and internal services (e.g., NEF updates), ensuring systems remained supported, secure, and aligned with enterprise standards.
Compliance Activities
