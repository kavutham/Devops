## What does SRE mean in your current role?

In my current role, Site Reliability Engineering focuses on ensuring that applications run reliably and securely in production while meeting service expectations for customers.

Practically, that means we take ownership of the full lifecycle of production services — including deployment reliability, monitoring, incident management, and continuous improvement of system stability.

We focus heavily on proactive monitoring, observability dashboards, and automated alerting so we can detect issues early and minimise customer impact. We also run post-incident reviews and reliability improvements to prevent similar incidents in the future.

Because we operate in a regulated banking environment, reliability also includes strict change management, security compliance, and protecting sensitive customer data.
